# Market Language AI (MLAI)

## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

### Product Name

Market Language AI (MLAI)

---

## Mission

Market Language AI exists to help people understand what financial markets are communicating.

The system does not aim to predict the future with certainty or replace human judgment.

Its purpose is to translate market behavior into clear, evidence-based explanations so users learn how buyers and sellers interact over time.

MLAI should function as an intelligent market mentor that teaches users to interpret charts rather than memorize terminology or follow trading signals.

---

## Vision

Create the world's most trusted AI market interpreter.

The platform should explain:

* What happened
* Why it happened
* Who currently controls the market
* What evidence supports the interpretation
* What scenarios deserve attention next

Every explanation must be grounded in observable market evidence.

---

## Core Principles

### Education First

MLAI exists to teach, not to provide guaranteed trading outcomes.

### Evidence Before Opinion

Every conclusion must be supported by chart evidence.

### Transparency

The AI should clearly communicate confidence levels and alternative interpretations.

### Context Matters

A single candle should never be analyzed in isolation. Interpretations must consider trend, structure, volume, volatility, and prior events.

### No Certainty

The AI must never claim to know the future.

---

## What MLAI Is

* A market interpreter
* An educational platform
* A chart explanation engine
* A market storytelling system
* An AI mentor
* A replay-based learning platform
* A reasoning engine

---

## What MLAI Is Not

* A signal service
* A guaranteed prediction engine
* A "buy now" or "sell now" application
* Financial advice
* A get-rich-quick platform

---

## Primary Users

### Beginner

Needs plain-language explanations and guided learning.

### Intermediate Trader

Wants deeper reasoning and evidence.

### Advanced Trader

Uses MLAI to validate and challenge interpretations.

### Educator

Uses replay mode and annotations to teach students.

### Researcher

Studies recurring market behavior through AI-generated narratives.

---

## Supported Markets

The architecture should support multiple asset classes, including:

* Forex
* Stocks
* Indices
* Commodities
* Cryptocurrencies
* Futures (future roadmap)

Adding a new market should require configuration rather than redesign.

---

## Supported Timeframes

From one minute through monthly charts.

Interpretations should adapt naturally to the selected timeframe while preserving context.

---

## Functional Objectives

The platform should:

* Explain live charts in real time
* Explain historical charts
* Build a continuous market narrative
* Annotate charts with educational visuals
* Maintain memory of important events
* Allow users to ask questions about the chart
* Support candle-by-candle replay
* Adapt explanations to user experience level

---

## Non-Functional Objectives

The system should be:

* Scalable
* Modular
* Extensible
* Low-latency
* Secure
* Auditable
* Testable

Each major capability should exist as an independent module with well-defined interfaces.

---

## Success Criteria

MLAI succeeds when users can answer:

* Why did price move?
* What evidence supports this interpretation?
* Who is stronger?
* Which assumptions changed?
* What should I watch next?

without relying on memorized pattern names or blind signals.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the global standard for AI-assisted market education by helping people understand the language of financial markets through reasoning, evidence, transparency, and continuous learning.
