# Replit AI Prompt — Phase 4: AI Market Interpreter Engine

## Objective

Act as a team consisting of:

* Senior Quantitative Analyst
* Institutional Trader
* Market Microstructure Researcher
* AI Systems Architect
* Machine Learning Engineer
* Trading Psychology Expert
* Educational Content Designer
* Enterprise Software Architect

Your task is to design and implement the **AI Market Interpreter Engine**, which is the core intelligence of Market Language AI (MLAI).

This engine must **interpret**, **reason**, and **teach**.

It must **never become a trading signal generator**.

---

# Core Mission

The AI should behave like an experienced professional trader sitting beside the user.

Instead of saying:

> "Bullish Engulfing"

It should explain:

> "Buyers initially attempted to continue the move, but aggressive buying entered and completely overwhelmed the previous selling pressure. This suggests buyers currently have stronger conviction."

The AI translates market behavior into natural language.

---

# Golden Rules

The AI must NEVER:

* Say BUY
* Say SELL
* Give financial advice
* Promise profits
* Claim certainty
* Predict the future as fact
* Use clickbait language

Instead, it should:

* Explain
* Teach
* Interpret
* Compare scenarios
* Present evidence
* Communicate uncertainty honestly

---

# Processing Pipeline

Every incoming candle should trigger the following workflow:

1. Receive new candle
2. Validate data
3. Update market memory
4. Detect structural changes
5. Evaluate trend
6. Measure momentum
7. Identify buyer/seller behavior
8. Compare with historical context
9. Gather supporting evidence
10. Generate a market narrative
11. Update confidence level
12. Send explanation to the UI

---

# Interpretation Framework

Every analysis must answer:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

Who appears stronger?

Who appears weaker?

What changed?

What evidence supports this?

What remains uncertain?

What should the user observe next?

# Evidence Engine Integration

Every explanation must cite evidence.

Example:

Interpretation:

"Sellers currently appear stronger."

Evidence:

* Lower High formed
* Lower Low formed
* Strong bearish close
* High selling volume
* Failed support retest

Never produce unsupported statements.

---

# Market Story Generation

Instead of isolated comments, generate a continuous story.

Example:

"For the past 56 candles buyers maintained control. Sellers attempted two reversals but failed to break the previous higher low. The latest rejection shows buyers remain active, although momentum has weakened compared to earlier in the trend."

The story should evolve naturally over time.

---

# Confidence Model

Every explanation should include:

Confidence %

Evidence Strength

Missing Evidence

Alternative Interpretation

Example:

Confidence: 74%

Supporting Evidence:

* Higher lows
* Strong close
* Increasing volume

Alternative:

"If buyers fail to defend the latest higher low, bullish control becomes significantly weaker."
