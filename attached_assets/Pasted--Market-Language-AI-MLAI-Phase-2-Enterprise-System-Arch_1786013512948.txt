# Market Language AI (MLAI)

# Phase 2 — Enterprise System Architecture

---

# Objective

Design a modular, enterprise-grade architecture capable of scaling from a single-user educational platform to millions of concurrent users while maintaining low latency, reliability, and maintainability.

The architecture must separate concerns so each component can evolve independently.

# Core Design Principles

Every service has one responsibility.

Every module communicates through APIs.

No module directly depends on another module's internal implementation.

Every service can be upgraded independently.

# Story Engine

The Story Engine converts isolated observations into a continuous narrative.

Instead of

"Candle is bullish"

It explains

"The market has remained under buyer control for the last 84 candles. Sellers attempted two reversals but failed to break support. Momentum is slowing, although buyers still maintain structural control."

# Replay Engine

One of the most important services.

Rules

Never access future candles.

Reveal one candle at a time.

Recompute analysis after every candle.

Allow

Pause

Forward

Backward

Speed adjustment

Bookmarks

Lessons

Replay is deterministic.

Running the same replay twice should produce the same interpretation.

# Architecture Philosophy

Every module should answer one question well.

The Market Data Service knows what happened.

The Evidence Engine knows why it matters.

The Market Memory Engine knows what happened before.

The Story Engine connects the events.

The Annotation Engine visualizes the evidence.

The Conversation Engine teaches the user.

Together, these components transform raw market data into understandable market language.
