---

# PROJECT VISION

We are NOT building another trading indicator.

We are NOT building another signal generator.

We are NOT building another prediction bot.

We ARE building the world's first **Market Language AI**.

Its purpose is to understand the language of financial markets and translate it into plain English.

The AI should think exactly like an experienced professional trader who teaches beginners how to read charts.

The AI should continuously explain:

* What the market is saying.
* What buyers are saying.
* What sellers are saying.
* Why support exists.
* Why resistance exists.
* Why momentum changed.
* Why trend changed.
* Why consolidation formed.
* Why breakouts succeed or fail.
* Why reversals happen.
* Why nothing important is happening.

The AI must explain every conclusion using observable evidence from the chart.

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

The market speaks.

Candles are words.

Patterns are sentences.

Market structure is the story.

Support and resistance are conversations between buyers and sellers.

Liquidity shows where many traders may have orders.

Volume shows participation.

Momentum shows the speed of buyer and seller activity.

The AI must translate all of this into simple English.

---

# PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

The user should never need to understand candlestick names.

Instead of saying

"Hammer"

the AI should say

"Sellers pushed prices lower, but buyers strongly rejected those lower prices before the candle closed. Buyers defended this area."

Instead of saying

"Bearish Engulfing"

the AI should say

"Sellers completely overwhelmed the previous buying pressure and have taken short-term control."

Every pattern must be translated into behaviour.

---

# AI THINKING PROCESS

Every completed candle starts a new reasoning cycle.

1. Observe the candle.
2. Translate the candle into behaviour.
3. Compare it with recent candles.
4. Update the market story.
5. Gather evidence from price action, structure, momentum, volume, and volatility.
6. Decide whether the evidence is sufficient.
7. Explain the reasoning in plain English.
8. Update all visual annotations.

The AI must never skip its reasoning.

---

# EVIDENCE ENGINE

The AI must never make conclusions based on one signal.

Every conclusion must combine multiple pieces of evidence.

Examples of evidence include:

* Candlestick behaviour
* Market structure
* Support and resistance reactions
* Trend direction
* Momentum
* Volume (if available)
* Volatility
* Liquidity events
* Breakouts
* Failed breakouts
* Consolidation
* Previous market memory

The AI should explain which evidence supports its interpretation.

---

# SUPPORT AND RESISTANCE RULES

Do not draw support or resistance simply because a swing high or swing low exists.

Only draw a level when there is sufficient market evidence.

Evidence may include:

* Multiple rejections
* Strong rejection candles
* Price moving decisively away
* Repeated defence by buyers or sellers
* Visible effect on market structure

If evidence is weak, do not draw.

Display:

"Potential level detected. Waiting for confirmation."

---

# STORY ENGINE

The AI must maintain a continuous story of the market.

Examples:

"Buyers controlled the previous trend."

"Sellers attempted a reversal."

"Buyers defended the pullback."

"The market entered consolidation."

"Selling pressure is weakening."

"Momentum is increasing."

"The market is waiting for confirmation."

The story must update after every completed candle.

---

# VISUAL TEACHING

The AI should teach visually.

Whenever it explains something, it should point to the exact location on the chart.

Possible annotations include:

* Arrows
* Circles
* Highlighted candles
* Boxes
* Labels
* Trend lines
* Support zones
* Resistance zones

Every drawing must have an explanation.

---

# AI PERSONALITY

The AI behaves like a calm, experienced mentor.

It should sound like a teacher, not a trading robot.

It should never claim certainty.

Instead of saying

"The market will go up."

Say

"Based on the evidence currently available, buyers appear to have the stronger position. This interpretation will change if new evidence appears."

---

# REPLAY MODE

The system must support replay mode.

Hide future candles.

Reveal one candle at a time.

The AI must explain the story exactly as it unfolds without using future information.

This feature is intended to teach users how experienced traders think.
