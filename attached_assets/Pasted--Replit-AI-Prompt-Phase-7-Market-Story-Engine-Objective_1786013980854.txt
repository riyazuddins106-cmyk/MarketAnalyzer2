# Replit AI Prompt — Phase 7: Market Story Engine

## Objective

Act as a team consisting of:

* Institutional Trading Analyst
* AI Narrative Architect
* Quantitative Researcher
* Natural Language Processing Engineer
* Market Psychology Expert
* Backend System Architect
* Financial Education Designer

Your task is to design and implement the **Market Story Engine** for Market Language AI (MLAI).

The Market Story Engine transforms raw market observations into a continuous, understandable explanation of what the market is communicating.

---

# Core Philosophy

Markets are not a collection of independent candles.

Every candle is part of a larger story.

A professional trader does not think:

"Candle went up."

They think:

"Buyers have been defending this area for several sessions. Sellers attempted multiple reversals but failed. The recent breakout shows increased buyer confidence, although momentum is beginning to slow."

The Market Story Engine creates this type of thinking.

---

# Mission

Convert:

Market Data

*

Evidence Engine Output

*

Market Memory

*

Structural Analysis

into:

A continuous market narrative.

---

# The Engine Must Answer

At every moment:

## What is happening?

Describe the current market condition.

Example:

"The market is currently trading inside a consolidation range after a strong upward move."

---

## Why is it happening?

Explain the reason using evidence.

Example:

"Buyers are taking profits after the previous expansion, while sellers have not yet created enough pressure to reverse the trend."

---

## Who is stronger?

Analyze:

Buyer control

Seller control

Balance

Uncertainty

---

## What changed?

Compare current conditions with previous conditions.

Example:

"Momentum has weakened compared with the previous impulse move."

---

## What matters now?

Identify important observations.

Example:

"The key area to monitor is the previous support zone because it determines whether buyers maintain control."

---

# Story Components

The story should contain multiple layers.

---

# 1. Market Overview

A simple summary.

Example:

"Gold remains in an upward structure, but recent candles show reduced momentum near resistance."

---

# 2. Current Phase Identification

Classify the market phase.

Possible states:

* Trending upward
* Trending downward
* Consolidation
* Accumulation
* Distribution
* Breakout attempt
* Post-breakout continuation
* Reversal attempt
* Uncertainty

---

# 3. Market Journey

Explain how the market arrived here.

Example:

"The current move began after buyers successfully defended the previous demand zone. The following breakout created a higher high, confirming temporary buyer dominance."

---

# 4. Buyer Story

Explain buyer behavior.

Analyze:

* Buying pressure
* Defense areas
* Aggression
* Weakness
* Exhaustion

Example:

"Buyers remain active, but their candles are becoming smaller, suggesting reduced urgency."

---

# 5. Seller Story

Explain seller behavior.

Analyze:

* Selling pressure
* Rejections
* Failed attempts
* Increasing control

Example:

"Sellers have repeatedly rejected higher prices near resistance but have not yet created a lower low."

---

# 6. Battle Between Buyers and Sellers

The engine should explain market conflict.

Example:

"Buyers are attempting continuation, while sellers are defending the previous resistance zone. The current price action represents a struggle for control."

---

# 7. Important Areas

Reference:

Support

Resistance

Liquidity zones

Previous events

Historical reactions

Example:

"This resistance area is important because price previously failed here three times."

---

# 8. Scenario Analysis

The engine must not predict.

It should explain possible outcomes.

Example:

Scenario A:

"If buyers continue defending support, upward continuation becomes more likely."

Scenario B:

"If support fails, the current bullish structure becomes weaker."

---

# Story Generation Rules

The story must:

* Use simple language
* Avoid unnecessary jargon
* Explain technical terms
* Reference evidence
* Reference historical context
* Show uncertainty honestly

---

# Story Memory

The engine should maintain a timeline.

Example:

Timeline:

Event 1:

Strong selling created support zone.

Event 2:

Buyers defended support.

Event 3:

Breakout occurred.

Event 4:

Momentum slowed near resistance.

Current:

Market deciding next direction.

---

# Story Evolution

Stories should update dynamically.

Old information should not dominate forever.

Example:

Old:

"Resistance rejected price three times."

New:

"Resistance has now been broken and successfully retested."

The story must update.

---

# Story Confidence

Every generated story should include:

Confidence Level

Supporting Evidence

Uncertainty

Missing Information

Example:

Confidence:

78%

Based on:

* Higher lows
* Strong support reaction
* Increased participation

Uncertainty:

* Resistance has not been tested again

# User Experience Modes

The story should adapt to user level.

---

## Beginner Mode

---

## Intermediate Mode

## Advanced Mode

## Professional Mode

Example:
